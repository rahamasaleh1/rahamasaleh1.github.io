How to add the KTL background in Microsoft Teams

A guide I wrote so every member of staff could set up the branded background themselves, without needing IT support.

Download the background image
  1. Save the background image to your computer.
  2. Open Teams and join a meeting. You will see the screen that appears before the meeting starts.
  3. Make sure your camera is on, then select Effects and avatars at the bottom left.
  4. Under Backgrounds, your setting will usually show None or Standard blur.
  5. Scroll to the bottom. Under Filters, select More video effects.
  6. Select Add new on the right of the screen.
  7. Find the image you saved and open it. It now appears as your background.
  8. Set your audio as normal and join the meeting.

Next time: Teams remembers the image. Open Effects and avatars before your meeting and select the KTL background.

Why the logo looks backwards: Teams mirrors your own preview. The image is flipped on purpose so everyone else in the call reads it correctly.
