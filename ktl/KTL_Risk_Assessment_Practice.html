Key information (HSE & KTL)

HSE – 5 steps

  1. Identify hazards.
  2. Decide who might be harmed and how.
  3. Evaluate risks (likelihood × severity) and choose precautions.
  4. Record findings (existing and further controls).
  5. Review regularly and after changes/incidents.

KTL essentials

  • Use KTL Placement RA; use Lone Working RA where relevant.
  • Review at least every 6 months or after significant change; manager sign-off required.
  • Explicitly cover: violence/aggression (verbal, threats, physical/sexual, weapons), substance misuse, missing episodes, mental health, environmental & lone-working risks.
  • Follow Local Safe Working Procedures; use buddy/check-in systems when lone working.
