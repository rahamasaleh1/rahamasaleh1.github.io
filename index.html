Training tools I built at Key Transitions

Key Transitions helps young people aged 18 to 25, including asylum seekers, move into independent living. While working there in admin and IT support, I noticed staff were learning from static documents and many of the young people needed support in their own language. So I built three interactive tools to help.

  • 3 tools built
  • 13 languages
  • Promoted within 3 months
  • Signed off by management before rollout

For support staff

Case Notes Training

Helps staff write clear, factual case notes. They work through real scenarios, draft a note and get instant feedback on anything missing.

Try Case Notes Training

For support staff and managers

Risk Assessment Practice

Walks staff through a risk assessment using the HSE five steps. Each risk is scored and colour coded as they go, and anything high risk is flagged for a manager to approve.

Try Risk Assessment Practice

For the young people KTL supports

KTL Home Skills

A drag and drop game that teaches everyday tasks like laundry, recycling and kitchen hygiene. It works in 13 languages, so young people can learn in the language they know best.
